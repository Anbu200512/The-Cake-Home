Our Story

Made With Love Since 2020

The Cake Home began in our kitchen in Hosur with one oven, one mixer and a simple belief: a cake should taste like someone cared. Since 2020 we have been baking small batches of fresh homemade cakes for birthdays, anniversaries and everyday celebrations.

Every cake is made to order with real butter, pure cream and seasonal fruit — never powdered shortcuts, never stored leftovers. That is why our orders fill up early on weekends.
